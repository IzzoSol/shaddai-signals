    1 · System architecture

    Three legs behind a public edge. The payments leg is mirrored from the private monolith (Shaddai-3d · feat/sap-treasury); every arrow into money ends at an intent, never an execution.

    2 · Agent council → Asimov

    SHADDAI orchestrates six specialists. Asimov is QUILL's first sub-agent: writing and education for regenerative, physical-world work. PIKADON reviews Asimov's copy against R7.

    3 · Fee & treasury flow

    Two ledgers that never touch. Real money is counted in cents; the x402 fee is SHAD-Credits, split once by splitByPolicy, and lands as intents waiting for a settler that deliberately doesn't exist.

    4 · HIP-1 deployment planner

    maxSupply and genesis allocation are two views of one number: state either and the other is derived; state both differently and it is a 400. The validator is never relaxed.

    5 · Growth model (EduTwin)

    The exact pipeline the Simulation view runs, per tree, then scaled by survivors. Change a scenario input and every chart re-derives from these formulas.

    6 · NVIDIA admission rule

    A repo enters only if it runs on this machine, or is knowledge/instruction-only. The verdict row is written before anything is installed.
