c. 8000 BCE · Fertile Crescent

The cat chooses us

Farmers stored grain. Grain drew mice. Mice drew the African wildcat, Felis lybica. DNA points to this wildcat as the ancestor of every house cat alive today.

On Cyprus, at Shillourokambos, a cat was buried beside a person about 9,500 years ago. Cats aren't native to the island, so someone brought it by boat.

Driscoll et al., Science, 2007. Vigne et al., Science, 2004.

c. 3700 BCE – 30 BCE · Egypt

Gods among us

Egypt raised the cat higher than anyone, before or since.

  • Bastet: a goddess of home, joy and protection, shown as a cat or a cat-headed woman. Her city was Bubastis.
  • The festival: Herodotus describes huge crowds sailing to Bubastis to celebrate her.
  • Mourning: he also reports that when a house cat died, the family shaved their eyebrows.
  • The law of the mob: Diodorus says a Roman who killed a cat in Egypt was lynched, even with the king urging mercy.
  • Ta-Miu: a prince, Thutmose, gave his cat a carved stone sarcophagus around 1350 BCE.

Cats were also mummified by the hundreds of thousands as offerings. In 1888, a find at Beni Hasan was so large that about 19 tons of mummies were shipped to England, largely to be ground up as fertilizer.

500 – 1300 · Across the world

Sailors, poets and courtiers

  • Norse myth: the goddess Freyja rides a chariot pulled by cats.
  • Japan: cats are thought to have arrived with Buddhist scriptures from China, guarding them from mice. Emperor Ichijo's cat was given a court rank around the year 1000, as The Pillow Book records.
  • Islamic tradition: stories hold that the Prophet Muhammad loved cats, including one named Muezza.
  • Ships: cats rode trade routes as mouse control, and spread around the world with them.
1233 – 1700s · Europe

The dark years

In 1233, Pope Gregory IX's letter Vox in Rama described a devil appearing as a black cat in a heretic rite. Black cats picked up a reputation they still haven't shaken.

In the witch-hunting centuries that followed, cats were often treated as a witch's familiar. Some were killed for it, and France's Saint John's Eve fires are among the recorded cruelty.

The popular claim that killing cats caused the Black Death is a later theory, and it's disputed.

1700s – 1963 · Rediscovered

Back in the house

  • Science and letters: Samuel Johnson bought oysters for his cat Hodge. Isaac Newton's cat-flap story is a legend, but it won't die.
  • 1871: Harrison Weir holds the first major modern cat show, at the Crystal Palace in London.
  • 1935: Schrödinger's thought experiment makes a cat the face of quantum physics.
  • 1963: Félicette, a French street cat, flies to space and returns alive.
2005 – Today · The internet

The cats won

"I Can Has Cheezburger?" launches in 2007. Keyboard Cat, filmed in 1984, goes viral. Nyan Cat arrives in 2011. Grumpy Cat becomes a global star in 2012.

In 2017, CryptoKitties gets so popular it clogs the Ethereum network. In 2021, the original Nyan Cat GIF sells as an NFT for about 300 ETH.

By then, cats had their own coins.
