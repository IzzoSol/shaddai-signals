Why a cat named Asimov

The man who wrote the laws for robots. The animal humans have trusted for four thousand years. The company building the robots' brains.

Asimov, editor-in-chief.

  1. c. 2000 BC · Egypt

    Sacred

    Cats belonged to the goddess Bastet. When a house cat died, Herodotus wrote, the family shaved their eyebrows in mourning.

  2. c. 1860s · Smiljan

    The first spark

    A boy stroked his cat Mačak on a dry winter night and its back lit up with sparks. Nikola Tesla grew up to light the world. CBC

  3. 1861 · The White House

    Presidents and prime ministers

    Abraham Lincoln brought cats into the White House. Winston Churchill's Jock was at Chartwell, where a marmalade cat named Jock still lives. Larry has been Chief Mouser at 10 Downing Street since 2011.

  4. 1942 · New York

    The laws

    Isaac Asimov wrote the Three Laws of Robotics. He loved cats too, and co-edited the anthologies Magicats! and Magicats II.

  5. 2004 · Chicago, 2035

    The witness

    In I, Robot, the one survivor of Dr. Lanning's house is a tabby with a red collar. Reportedly, its name is Asimov. Cinema Cats

  6. 2011 · The internet

    The mascot

    Nyan Cat, Grumpy Cat, a billion videos. Cats became the face of the internet, then the face of crypto.

  7. 2012 · Google Brain

    AI's first word

    A neural network watched millions of YouTube frames with no labels and taught itself to recognise… a cat. A year later Stanford and NVIDIA rebuilt it 6.5× bigger on GPUs. insideHPC

  8. 2017 · NVIDIA

    Isaac

    NVIDIA launched Isaac, its robotics platform, reportedly named after Isaac Asimov. Today it trains the robots his laws were written for. 2017 report

  9. Now · Solana

    $ASIMOV × NVDAx

The Three Laws

In 1942, Isaac Asimov wrote three laws for robots that didn't exist yet. The robots showed up. The laws are still standing. This is the token for everyone who saw it coming.

  1. A robot may not injure a human being or, through inaction, allow a human being to come to harm.He wrote it in 1942. Now the robots are real and NVIDIA builds the chips that train them. Asimov called it first.
  2. A robot must obey the orders given it by human beings except where such orders would conflict with the First Law.Robots take orders. The cat takes orders from no one. Diamond paws.
  3. A robot must protect its own existence as long as such protection does not conflict with the First or Second Law.So should you. The team will never message you first, and nobody legitimate will ever ask for your seed phrase.

Featured feline

Case file: the cat in I, Robot, and the cats behind science fiction.

Reportedly: ASIMOV

Exhibit A · Chicago, 2035

A cat named Asimov

In I, Robot (2004), Del Spooner walks into Dr. Lanning's empty house and finds one witness: a tabby-and-white cat with a red collar. Minutes later a demolition robot starts tearing the house down around them. Spooner carries the cat out, then leaves it with his grandmother, Gigi.

The film never says the cat's name. The trivia files say it's Asimov.

Asimov was a cat lover too. According to Cinema Cats, he and his wife, the writer Janet Jeppson Asimov, kept cats at home, and cats turn up in his essays on animal intelligence. He also co-edited the cat anthologies Magicats! (1984) and Magicats II (1991). That's where his essay "I Love Little Pussy" appeared. Our editor-in-chief wears the red collar in his honour.

Sources: Cinema Cats, I, Robot (2004) · Wikipedia

The fellowship of writers and cats

Robert A. HeinleinPixie & Pixel

Pixie searched every door in the house for one that led away from the snow, "a door into summer." It became the novel The Door into Summer. Pixel later walked into The Cat Who Walks Through Walls.

Philip K. DickPinky

Dick came to believe Pinky was his late mentor Anthony Boucher, reborn. The cat runs through The Exegesis.

Ursula K. Le GuinPard

Pard sat on her desk while she thought about time and Schrödinger's cat. He starred in her Annals of Pard.

Terry PratchettPatch

Patch slept in a desk drawer while Pratchett wrote. The cats got a book of their own, The Unadulterated Cat, and Greebo got Discworld.

H. G. WellsPeter

In First and Last Things, Peter sleeps on the window seat a few steps from Wells's desk. He's no symbol, just company while Wells thinks.

H. P. LovecraftThe cats of Ulthar

"In Ulthar, which lies beyond the river Skai, no man may kill a cat." The Cats of Ulthar, 1920.

Neil GaimanThe black cat

A real black cat turned up one day and became the hero of "The Price."

Ray BradburyOn creativity

"You treat ideas like cats: you make them follow you."

After Jacques Julien's essay Cats and Science Fiction Authors. No cat was harmed in the making of this token.
